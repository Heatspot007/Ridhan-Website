The doctors

Because most visitors arrive already knowing a name, the people come before anything else on this page.

Dr. G. Aravindan

MBBS, MD (DVL)

Consultant Dermatologist

Trained at Madras Medical College

One consultant for the full range of skin disease — psoriasis, vitiligo, acne, fungal infection, urticaria, autoimmune conditions and venereal disease — alongside facial reconstruction and minor skin surgery carried out at the clinic.

His practice is evidence-based. The treatment follows the diagnosis, and the reasoning behind it is explained to you before anything begins.

  • Psoriasis
  • Vitiligo
  • Acne & scarring
  • Dermatophytosis
  • Autoimmune skin disease
  • Urticaria
  • Venereal disease
  • Facial reconstruction
  • Hair loss
  • Minor surgery

Dr. Kiruuthika KA

MBBS · DCH · DNB (Paediatrics)

Consultant Paediatrician

MBBS, Kilpauk Medical College · DCH, Thanjavur Medical College · DNB, Dr. Mehta's Hospital

She looks for the cause rather than treating the symptom in front of her — which is why parents tend to leave understanding what was actually wrong.

Her particular interest is developmental paediatrics: how a child is growing, feeding, moving and learning, not only whether they are unwell today.

  • Developmental paediatrics
  • Newborn care
  • Growth & development
  • Vaccination
  • Asthma
  • Allergy
  • General consultation

What happens when you book

Appointments are confirmed by a person, never an automated calendar — so you always speak to someone before you travel.

01

You send a request

By phone, WhatsApp, or the form below. Your name, a number, and what it's about.

We agree a time that works and tell you which doctor you will be seeing.

An unhurried examination. Bring any previous prescriptions or reports.
